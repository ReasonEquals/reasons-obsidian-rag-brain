# 2024-01-15

## Session log

Spent most of the morning getting the chunker working correctly. The merge logic for short sections is trickier than it looks — you have to carry forward both the heading and the content, then decide whether to merge the carry into the *next* section or the *previous* one when you hit the end of the file.

Got the H2-split working cleanly. The slug deduplication for repeated headings (two `## Status` sections in the same file) was a good catch — without it, ChromaDB upsert silently drops the second one.

Afternoon: first real embedding run against the vault. Voyage API worked on the first try. The asymmetric input_type thing wasn't obvious from the docs — had to read the model card to understand that `query` and `document` aren't just labels, the model was specifically trained for this asymmetry. First test search ("what did I decide about chunking strategy") returned the right chunk immediately with a score of 0.28. Strong match.

## Capture

- ChromaDB's `get()` with `include=[]` is the right way to pull just IDs for incremental hash comparison — don't include documents or embeddings if you don't need them
- The `PRAGMA busy_timeout` on SQLite is important when the sync script and index script might both be running; without it you get "database is locked" errors
- Rate limit handling needs exponential backoff, not linear — Voyage's 429 responses come in bursts when you exceed TPM, and a fixed delay often just re-triggers the limit
